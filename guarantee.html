Our Guarantee

Genuine products

Everything we sell comes from licensed wholesalers and manufacturers, never from the grey market. Each product is stored as its label says and checked for its expiry date before it is packed.

If something goes wrong

If your order arrives damaged, incomplete or not what you ordered, tell us within 14 days and we will replace it or refund you, including the delivery cost. Send a photo to demo@info.com or call the pharmacy, and keep the parcel until we have sorted it out.

Free advice

Not sure which product is right for you, or whether it mixes with something you already take? Our pharmacists will answer by phone or email, free, whether or not you buy from us.
