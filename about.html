About Us

Our story

MediStore started in 2009 as a small high-street pharmacy at 701 Old York Drive, and the online shop grew out of the questions our regulars kept asking: can you post this to me? Today we send vitamins, everyday medicines and health essentials across the country, and the counter is still open for anyone who would rather talk to us in person.

We are a registered pharmacy (registration number 1234567). Every order is checked by a member of the pharmacy team before it leaves the building.

The team
  • Sarah Patel, pharmacist and owner. Sarah answers most of the medicine questions that come in by phone and email.
  • Tom Reyes, pharmacist. Tom looks after the vitamins and supplements range and checks every order before it is packed.
  • Lena Novak, dispatch. Lena packs the parcels and sorts out anything that goes astray.
Opening hours
  • Monday to Friday: 8:30 to 18:30
  • Saturday: 9:00 to 17:00
  • Sunday: closed
